DO'S & DON'TS FOR A

SAFE DIWALI

Safe Choices • Happy Celebrations • Brighter Tomorrow

Do's

Keep a bucket of water beside you while bursting crackers.

Ensure that you have a first aid kit.

Ignite crackers only in open outdoor areas.

Immerse used crackers in water before disposal.

Keep children indoors after bursting crackers safely.

Don'ts

Never experiment with crackers.

Don't leave children unattended while bursting crackers.

Don't wear nylon clothes while bursting crackers.

Don't burst crackers near electric poles and wires.

Don't keep your face close while lighting crackers.
