Safety First

Celebrate Responsibly

Follow these simple precautions for a safe and memorable Diwali celebration.

Maintain Distance

Keep at least 15 feet away from lit fireworks and never lean over them.

Keep Water Ready

Always keep a bucket of water or a fire extinguisher nearby.

Adult Supervision

Children should burst crackers only under adult supervision.

Never Relight

Do not relight failed fireworks. Wait and soak them in water.

Store Properly

Store crackers in a cool, dry place away from heat and flames.

Wear Cotton Clothes

Wear cotton clothes and avoid loose synthetic clothing.
